Responsible AI Considerations

  • Fairness: Rules apply uniformly across all zones — no zone is assumed "high risk" by default.
  • Transparency: Every alert includes a plain-language reason. No black-box scoring — all thresholds are visible and explainable.
  • Ethics: Flags support human review and decision-making; the system does not take autonomous action (e.g. shutting off water) without a human in the loop.
  • Privacy: Detection works on zone-level aggregate flow data only — no individual household or personal usage data is collected or processed.
